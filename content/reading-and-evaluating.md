These are my working notes on building and understanding systems that combine people, software, data, and AI. They cover how responsibilities are shared, how information flows, and how to find out whether the system does useful work.

[Evaluation]({{< ref "ai-engineering/evaluation" >}}) is a main focus: choosing cases, recording what happened, understanding failures, and deciding what to change. The [AI section]({{< ref "ai" >}}) explores product design, human–AI collaboration, interfaces, and research questions.

I publish the notes as I develop the ideas. Some are practical guides or worked examples; others contain questions, design discussions, and unfinished arguments. I share them so others can use the ideas, question the reasoning, and discuss problems that are still open.

Some pages preserve conversations with language models. Speaker labels distinguish my questions and responses from the model's. A model's proposal may still be under discussion.

The [reading map]({{< ref "research" >}}) lists the main topics, and the [agent map](/agent-map/) provides a compact index of the site.
