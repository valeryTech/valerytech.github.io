Exploratory pipeline design uses inspectable artifacts, interaction, and progressive automation to learn about a pipeline before committing to a fixed implementation. The linked notes develop three formulations of this approach. The third remains a proposal for analysis rather than an accepted method.

- Exploratory pipeline design and progressive automation covers the framing, interaction criteria, anti-patterns, and progressive-automation method.
- Inspectable pipeline workbenches describes an artifact model, RAG example, runtime and design-time interactions, and architecture.
- Exploratory pipeline design goals and principles sets out the proposed purpose, goals, non-goals, principles, operating loops, objective, and manifesto.

The separate method comments, workbench comments, and comments on goals and principles identify open questions. The stage 9 audit records every original line and checksum. Research descriptions, product and metric claims, and references to unavailable attachments were not independently checked during that reorganization.
