Exploratory pipeline design uses inspectable artifacts, interaction, and progressive automation to learn about a pipeline before committing to a fixed implementation. The linked notes develop three formulations of this approach. The third remains a proposal for analysis rather than an accepted method.

- [Exploratory pipeline design and progressive automation]({{< ref "ai/design-research/interactive-systems/10-exploratory-pipeline-design-and-progressive-automation" >}}) covers the framing, interaction criteria, anti-patterns, and progressive-automation method.
- [Inspectable pipeline workbenches]({{< ref "ai/design-research/interactive-systems/11-inspectable-pipeline-workbenches" >}}) describes an artifact model, RAG example, runtime and design-time interactions, and architecture.
- [Exploratory pipeline design goals and principles]({{< ref "ai/design-research/interactive-systems/12-exploratory-pipeline-design-goals-and-principles" >}}) sets out the proposed purpose, goals, non-goals, principles, operating loops, objective, and manifesto.

The separate [method comments]({{< ref "ai/design-research/interactive-systems/10-exploratory-pipeline-design-and-progressive-automation-comments" >}}), [workbench comments]({{< ref "ai/design-research/interactive-systems/11-inspectable-pipeline-workbenches-comments" >}}), and [comments on goals and principles]({{< ref "ai/design-research/interactive-systems/12-exploratory-pipeline-design-goals-and-principles-comments" >}}) identify open questions.
