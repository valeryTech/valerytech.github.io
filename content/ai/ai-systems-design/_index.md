This directory is the starting point for the next stage of the work. I intend to develop a coherent account of how to reason about systems that combine people, software, data, models, and LLMs: what work they support, how their parts contribute, and how people can understand and influence the resulting behavior.

The [introduction and reading map]({{< ref "ai/design-research/00-introduction" >}}) explains the purpose of the project and offers routes through the existing material. The [design-research collection]({{< ref "ai/design-research/00-index" >}}) holds the earlier exploration, including the arguments first developed under [architecture-dsl-like]({{< ref "ai/architecture-dsl-like/_index-dsl" >}}), alongside later dialogues, research reports, and critiques.

The intention here is to develop clearer concepts, explanations, and worked examples, then use them to formulate focused questions for implementation and research. Understanding the situation, comparing possibilities, and explaining design choices are part of that work. As it develops, I plan to produce a more finished set of notes and a set of papers.

This page establishes the continuation; substantive notes will be added as the work develops.
