## Original author observation


Author assessment: Hebbia appears highly creative. The following observations are offered for critique:

1. The 'Matrix' appears to provide an effective visual account of what is happening in the process.
2. It also appears to support managing the process and changing its parameters.
3. The work involves a whole 'pipeline', not one question. The author proposes broadening it into a collaborative investigation process that supports further questions and can be represented technically as a graph.
4. Open observation: Does the visual matrix give domain experts an interactive surface for performing and managing the process?

The author is enthusiastic about extending the matrix into hybrid UI structures assembled from blocks on demand, or representing those structures in a matrix. Although the product describes the work as research, the author proposes making it more collaborative, including teams of agents and multi-agent systems as well as people.
