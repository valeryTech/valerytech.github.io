## Original workflow sketch


<!-- Co-writer workflow source begins below. -->

## initial


take paragraph or text as input. example:

Author concern: Competence illusion can affect an experienced software engineer or architect who assumes that a couple of courses and conventional processes are enough to build a reliable AI product from scratch. A product engineer may similarly build a prototype without understanding the engineering effort required for production. Processes matter, and evaluations are a critical part of AI development.

1. separate to sentences and logical parts, for each part propose several alternatives
2. Proposed output assembly: Select preferred options for each part, or selected text within those options, and assemble the result. Open question: Would text selection be the right interaction?
3. assemble it to final text
