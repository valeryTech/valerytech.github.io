# Comments on the design research document map


Date: 2026-10-01. The comments are new editorial analysis for the [document map]({{< ref "ai/design-research/document-map" >}}). The relationships suggest ways to read the collection, and they do not select an architecture or establish a research result.

## Scope of the pass


The pass refreshes the saved map and reviews gaps in its conceptual relationships. It keeps the collection boundary and all retained notes in place. The interactive-systems collection remains a set of direct outside references until a separate mapping pass.

The earlier catalogue contained all 72 content notes. Its 45 conceptual relationships connected 46 notes, while 26 notes appeared only in the catalogue. The review considered the purpose and placement of all 26 entries. Checked passages support 36 additional relationships, which connect 25 of the entries to the conceptual map.

The review used selected discussion passages, report summaries, existing comments, and navigation added during earlier reassembly. It was not a complete rereading of every dialogue, report, or bibliography. The new relationships have 40 checked evidence passages, and none relies only on the collection index description.

The earlier 45 relationships remain unchanged in the authored definitions. Their evidence locations were refreshed by the builder. The presence of matching text confirms the location of a passage, but it does not automatically validate an interpretation of that passage.

No cited paper, product, legal claim, patent, or benchmark was independently verified. The saved reports remain attributed source material. The pass adds no implementation, experiment, or publication decision.

## Keep the original index as historical navigation


The [original index]({{< ref "ai/design-research/00-overview/03-original-index-notes" >}}) contains short pointers to behavioral types, type systems, and domain modeling. It remains the only content entry without an authored conceptual arrow.

The original index already has explicit outside references in the catalogue and snapshot. Adding an arrow merely to include every file would add little explanation. A later review may add a connection if a specific reading question needs one.

## Distinctions the new diagrams retain


The protocol diagram concerns permitted behavior and proposed execution properties. A permitted sequence does not establish that participants reached a shared understanding. A conditional invariant argument also does not prove an arbitrary model judgment.

The mediation diagram concerns separate design dimensions. A program can express an operation without authority to perform it. A sandbox can limit infrastructure access without deciding whether an allowed business operation is appropriate.

The captured-example diagram concerns the article's domain vocabulary and scenario language. The examples have not been run here, and compilation does not establish agreement with the requested experiment. A deterministic scenario also does not cover every possible execution of a distributed protocol.

The augmentation and investigation diagram connects historical reading, evolving information needs, and worked retrieval examples. Tests of evidence contracts do not measure user understanding by themselves. The proposed interaction study has no measured result in the collection.

The framing diagram shows the development of the question. The initial architectural possibilities remain available beside the later critique. The diagram does not make an architectural choice a requirement of the general theory.

## Navigation and maintenance


The [relationship definitions](/ai/design-research/document-map-comments.assets/document-map-relations.json) contain the authored connections and diagram selections. The [builder](/ai/design-research/document-map-comments.assets/build-design-research-map.py) refreshes evidence locations, file hashes, and both generated outputs without rewriting source notes.

The map comments are excluded from the content inventory and relationship extraction. The snapshot records the comments file's path and hash separately, so changes to the commentary can still make the saved snapshot stale. The exclusion also prevents the review's links from becoming evidence for its own conclusions.

The known heading warning remains in the [retained scope dialogue]({{< ref "ai/design-research/00-overview/06-research-scope-and-hierarchy" >}}#analyse-problem), where the original local reference is `[another](#another)`. The note's navigation already points to the [broader-principle passage]({{< ref "ai/design-research/01-foundations/01-constrained-generativity" >}}#another). The original reference is preserved instead of rewriting dialogue to remove a warning.

## Remaining work


First, prepare a companion map for the [interactive-systems collection]({{< ref "ai/design-research/interactive-systems/00-index" >}}). Then add explicit connections between the collections without merging their catalogues or treating every linked source as a design requirement.

Second, add navigation by design question. The useful questions concern agreement on intent, editable state, permitted operations, preserved properties, result use, and evaluation. Each question should identify its unresolved choices as well as its reading links.

Third, distinguish retained dialogue, author requirements, model proposals, captured examples, new analysis, and experiment plans where the distinction helps a reader. Labels may need to apply to sections because one note can contain several kinds of material. No blanket status is assigned to a whole dialogue by the current pass.
