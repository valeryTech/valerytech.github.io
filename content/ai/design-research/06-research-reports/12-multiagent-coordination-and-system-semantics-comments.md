## Coordination rules and their limits


The failure-to-primitive table proposes possible mechanisms. It does not establish that each mechanism is necessary, sufficient, or effective under the reported conditions. Ownership, resource limits, review, arbitration, and goal compatibility need defined participants, states, and enforcement conditions.

For example, an ownership record requires a rule about who can change the shared artifact and what happens when ownership expires. A review requirement also needs a distinction between recording an approval and checking that the reviewer examined the required evidence. The [framework comments]({{< ref "ai/design-research/05-runtime-and-execution/07-collaboration-framework-comments" >}}) discuss related enforcement limits.

The proposal separates orchestration, collaboration, and multiparty coordination. The separation is a proposed classification, rather than a required maturity sequence or a choice of implementation.
