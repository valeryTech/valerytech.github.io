## Claim types and evidence requirements


The observation, operational result, construct inference, mechanism, generalization, and forecast fields distinguish kinds of claims. They are not automatically calibrated levels of confidence or a sequence every result must pass through. Evidence for a measured result may leave its mechanism unresolved.

Testing understanding through prediction, intervention, counterfactual reasoning, and transfer is a proposed approach within the reply's working definitions. A later study needs specific tasks, comparison conditions, scoring rules, and competing explanations. Passing a proposed checklist would not establish that the interpretation is the only possible one.

The [provenance note]({{< ref "ai/design-research/04-investigation-and-epistemics/04-provenance-and-argumentation-foundations" >}}) concerns derivation records and support relations. A recorded source or exact quotation can show where a claim came from without showing that the source supports the claim. An evidence record should retain the difference between a source's observation and the researcher's inference.

## Proposed research practices and LLM use


The ten practices are proposed methods, not a completed experimental protocol. The reply selects no task set, participants, sample size, allocation procedure, measures, or analysis plan. The existing [collaboration]({{< ref "ai/design-research/06-research-reports/11-human-ai-collaboration-evaluation" >}}), [investigation]({{< ref "ai/design-research/06-research-reports/07-investigation-evaluation-and-benchmarks" >}}), and [runtime]({{< ref "ai/design-research/06-research-reports/05-agent-runtime-evaluation-and-benchmarks" >}}) evaluation notes have distinct questions and cannot replace one another's tests.

The proposed policy limits the use of LLM output as authority for factual or theoretical claims. LLM output can still be observed data in an experiment about LLM behavior. Generated synthesis is not independent evidence merely because another model repeats it, and a model's suggested counterargument does not resolve a disagreement between theories.

Sources, assumptions, alternative interpretations, and reasons to change a claim need inspectable records. A confidence label alone does not supply evidence or show that the confidence was calibrated.
