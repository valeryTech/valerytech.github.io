# Design research


Start with [the introduction and reading map]({{< ref "ai/design-research/00-introduction" >}}) for the purpose of the collection, its intended contribution to practice and understanding, and the relationship between the earlier DSL exploration and the continuation in [AI systems design]({{< ref "ai/ai-system-design" >}}). The detailed reading guide follows below.

Editorial context, 2026-04-20: The questions, constraints, proposals, disagreements, and caveats below are reformulated from the saved research discussion. LLM recommendations remain attributed proposals, not decisions by the author or independently verified findings. Technical examples and quoted source passages retain their original wording.

The note set reorganizes the former `architecture-dsl-like` collection while preserving its source arguments and technical detail through editorial reformulation. The reading path starts with the general theory, continues with the builder method, and then introduces applications.

The [research-scope note]({{< ref "ai/design-research/00-overview/06-research-scope-and-hierarchy" >}}) explains the hierarchy and separates investigation, behavioral compilation, and human collaboration. No first experiment or implementation is selected by the arrangement.

The notes preserve author questions, LLM proposals, and later critiques, with conversational wording edited for standalone reading. A recommendation in a retained dialogue is not automatically an accepted decision, and a cited literature claim has not been verified merely because it appears in the collection.

The sibling [collection comments]({{< ref "ai/design-research/00-index-comments" >}}) record unresolved choices, terminology distinctions, and source and navigation findings. They are new commentary, separate from the retained dialogues.

The [document map]({{< ref "ai/design-research/document-map" >}}) shows conceptual relationships, explicit links, sibling comments, and direct source and context references. Its arrows are navigation suggestions, not accepted decisions or proven dependencies.

Related dialogues from notes and the linked archive are being added one document at a time, while retaining their content with editorial wording. [Complexity and work structure]({{< ref "ai/design-research/01-foundations/10-complexity-and-work-structure" >}}) adds a proposed work profile. [Joint activity and collaboration]({{< ref "ai/design-research/02-human-ai-collaboration/05-joint-activity-and-collaboration-model" >}}) and [collaboration patterns]({{< ref "ai/design-research/02-human-ai-collaboration/06-human-ai-collaboration-patterns" >}}) add the related model and pattern discussions. Sibling comments distinguish the retained proposals from new interpretation.

The sibling [interactive systems collection]({{< ref "ai/design-research/interactive-systems/00-index" >}}) separates interface proposals from language, runtime, and evidence-contract research. Its first split keeps the Hebbia case in the project input folder and the retrieval-model dialogue in the investigation section below.

## Suggested reading path


1. [Research scope and hierarchy]({{< ref "ai/design-research/00-overview/06-research-scope-and-hierarchy" >}}) distinguishes the general theory, builder method, and applications.
2. [Constrained generativity]({{< ref "ai/design-research/01-foundations/01-constrained-generativity" >}}) develops the principle that restricting local behavior can support guarantees and composition.
3. [Constraint, guarantee, and composition models]({{< ref "ai/design-research/01-foundations/07-constraint-guarantee-and-composition-models" >}}) explores proposed formal models for the principle.
4. [Semantic substrate builder method]({{< ref "ai/design-research/01-foundations/08-semantic-substrate-builder-method" >}}) derives constraints and enforcement from desired guarantees. [Complexity and work structure]({{< ref "ai/design-research/01-foundations/10-complexity-and-work-structure" >}}) proposes an input to that method, not a validated complexity measure.
5. [Verification and acceptance]({{< ref "ai/design-research/01-foundations/09-verification-and-acceptance" >}}) distinguishes compiler checks, result verification, acceptance for a purpose, and authorization for external effects.
6. [Refined research question]({{< ref "ai/design-research/00-overview/05-refined-research-question" >}}) critiques assumptions in the earlier collaborative-environment question.
7. [Augmenting human work]({{< ref "ai/design-research/02-human-ai-collaboration/01-augmenting-human-work" >}}) and [sensemaking]({{< ref "ai/design-research/02-human-ai-collaboration/02-sensemaking-and-epistemic-action" >}}) examine collaboration and better understanding as outcomes. The [joint-activity model]({{< ref "ai/design-research/02-human-ai-collaboration/05-joint-activity-and-collaboration-model" >}}) distinguishes general mechanics from the specific [collaboration patterns]({{< ref "ai/design-research/02-human-ai-collaboration/06-human-ai-collaboration-patterns" >}}). The later [research synthesis]({{< ref "ai/design-research/02-human-ai-collaboration/07-collaboration-patterns-research-synthesis" >}}) links the expanded catalog and proposed [collaboration tests]({{< ref "ai/design-research/06-research-reports/11-human-ai-collaboration-evaluation" >}}). 11. [Participant-neutral system model]({{< ref "ai/design-research/01-foundations/04-participant-neutral-system-model" >}}) and [generalized agent runtime]({{< ref "ai/design-research/05-runtime-and-execution/01-generalized-agent-runtime" >}}) explore possible system architectures.

[Program versions, runs, and results]({{< ref "ai/design-research/05-runtime-and-execution/06-program-versions-runs-and-results" >}}) groups saved program versions, execution records, repairs, and decisions about using results.
