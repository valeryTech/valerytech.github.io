A catalog of applications in the B2B and professional sector, organized by the recurring job people do, for finding where an LLM product fits. It covers independent professionals, practices, firms, businesses, research organizations, and training providers. Client-facing products are included when a professional or organization supplies them.

Candidates are organized as **product family -> recurring job -> specialist variations**. Buyer descriptions name plausible budget owners to investigate rather than validated markets; the list is unranked.

## Product families


| Family | Main work |
| --- | --- |
| [1. Professional practice and client collaboration](#1-professional-practice-and-client-collaboration) | Help professionals and clients prepare, participate, and continue work together. |
| [2. Evidence review and case preparation](#2-evidence-review-and-case-preparation) | Examine records and prepare a review, finding, or application. |
| [3. Research, analysis, and professional decisions](#3-research-analysis-and-professional-decisions) | Investigate questions and compare evidence or alternatives. |

Creative businesses and broader platform offerings appear after these families.

## 1. Professional practice and client collaboration


**General product concept: a helper for professional work and the client relationship around it.** The profession is a specialization; the professional-side, client-side, and shared versions are separate product choices.

### Three product perspectives


| Product | Possible buyer | Users | Recurring job | Useful result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Professional-side workflow helper** | Professional, practice, or firm. | Professional and staff. | Understand a current case, prepare work, and manage unanswered questions and follow-up. | A usable case picture, prepared work, and clear outstanding tasks. |
| **Client-side helper** | Practice or service provider. | Client, patient, learner, or other service recipient. | Understand requests, provide information, explore their own concerns and options, and continue agreed work. | The client can contribute to the engagement and make progress on their own goals. |
| **Shared engagement assistant** | Practice or professional firm. | Professional, staff, and client. | Coordinate documents, decisions, responsibilities, progress, and follow-up. | Both sides understand the current state and next contribution. |

An independent client advocate is a separate choice from a practice-supplied participation helper. A professional advocacy service could buy an assistant for its own client work; a directly purchased consumer advocate remains in the broader pool.

### Workflow variations within these products


- **Onboarding and intake** -- Understand a new client's situation and prepare for the first encounter.
- **Information and document collection** -- Explain requests, collect useful material, and resolve missing information.
- **Case and work preparation** -- Bring together history, facts, questions, and material for the professional.
- **Reports and deliverables** -- Prepare briefs, recommendations, reports, proposals, and client communications.
- **Client explanation and follow-through** -- Help clients understand outputs and carry out agreed steps.
- **Client problem-solving and decision support** -- Help clients clarify their situation, explore options and trade-offs, and revisit whether an agreed approach helped.
- **Between-session continuity** -- Connect appointments with exercises, observations, and changes between them.
- **Client understanding and reflection** -- Revisit the shared picture, resolve contradictions, and adjust an approach.
- **Engagement coordination** -- Track responsibilities, requests, deadlines, decisions, and accepted work.
- **Practice-wide coordination** -- Help staff organize unfinished work across several clients or engagements.
- **Multi-professional coordination** -- Connect contributions when a case involves several specialists.
