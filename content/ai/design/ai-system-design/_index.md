How do representations affect what people and models can build? What must hold when components are combined? How can people understand and change the resulting system?

- [Representations, composition, and understanding]({{< ref "ai/design/ai-system-design/representations-composition-and-understanding" >}}) develops these questions and the proposed principles behind them.
- [Twenty research problems]({{< ref "ai/design/ai-system-design/research-problems-and-prototypes" >}}) proposes prototype comparisons to examine those principles. The studies are planned work.
- [Generated instruments]({{< ref "ai/design/generated-instruments" >}}) looks at the case where the model uses text generation to build an instrument (a diagram, page, simulation, or tool) instead of writing an answer.
- [Earlier design research]({{< ref "ai/design-research/00-introduction" >}}) links the source conversations, thematic notes, and critiques.
