# Discovery, prototyping, and sensemaking: document map


The focus is developing our understanding and mental models through exploration. LLMs can make possibilities concrete quickly: small implementations, interfaces, examples, and variations we can play with. A prototype can be disposable while the questions, distinctions, and knowledge it produces remain useful.

Comments below are my interpretation and suggested use of the existing notes (2026-10-19).

## Core discovery notes


- [Operation model]({{< ref "ai/discovery/analysis-or-operation-model" >}}) -- Start here for the working approach. It allows inquiry before a precise problem exists and recognizes better questions and visible possibilities as progress. The model remains provisional.
- [Exploratory design inquiry]({{< ref "ai/discovery/discovery-rewritten" >}}) -- The organized research synthesis behind that approach: framing, sensemaking, co-evolution, design-space exploration, and prototyping. Best for understanding the conceptual foundations.
- [LLM roles in discovery]({{< ref "ai/discovery/discovery-llm-roles" >}}) -- The most direct guidance for using LLMs in exploration. Give the LLM room to make possibilities inspectable and tryable; constructing an example need not establish our direction.
- [Dictionary]({{< ref "ai/discovery/dictionary" >}}) -- Clarifies what we are building: design knowledge, inquiry artifacts, and eventually a working system. Useful for distinguishing a prototype from the understanding it helps develop.
- [Discovery analysis]({{< ref "ai/discovery/discovery-analysis" >}}) -- An intermediate interpretation organizing the approach into foundations, evolving state, inquiry modes, and principles. Helpful commentary alongside the operation model.
- [Discovery research notes]({{< ref "ai/discovery/discovery" >}}) and [v0 backup]({{< ref "ai/discovery/discovery-v0-backup" >}}) -- Preserve the reasoning and examples behind the synthesis in standalone prose. The main note contains the key statement that the central object being developed is the person's model of the design situation. Use the backup for history.
- [Uncertainty ladder]({{< ref "ai/discovery/uncertainty-ladder" >}}) -- Distinguishes possibility, approach, feasibility, operation, and value. Useful for identifying what a probe can teach us; I would use these as lenses rather than mandatory stages.

## Sensemaking and interactive system design


- Sensemaking and epistemic action -- The clearest account of better mental models as an outcome of human-AI work. This gives conceptual language to the learning we seek through interaction.
- Augmentation literature and reading path -- Broader background on tools extending human understanding and collaborative investigation. A route into the literature rather than a prototyping procedure.
- Exploratory pipeline design and comments -- Apply exploration to uncertain pipeline decisions. Valuable for making behavior inspectable; progressive automation is one possible outcome of inquiry.
- Inspectable pipeline workbenches and comments -- Concrete proposals for artifacts, interventions, traces, and a RAG playground. Borrow the interaction ideas before committing to the proposed infrastructure.
- Exploratory pipeline goals and principles and comments -- A broader engineering proposal. Read the comments with it: recorded interventions still need interpretation and evidence before becoming general rules.
- Retrieval design -- A concrete application using small baselines, shared artifacts, and interactive comparisons. Particularly useful for turning our knowledge-search examples into something we can investigate.

## Adjacent perspectives


- Baseline, prototype, and feasibility -- Relevant when representative workflows and reproducible comparisons become necessary. Its engineering requirements should follow the purpose of the experiment.
- AI prototyping at Uber -- A practitioner account of broader exploration and shared understanding through interactive prototypes. Useful supporting experience; our notes develop the additional emphasis on personal sensemaking and mental models.

My suggested starting path: **operation model -> LLM roles -> sensemaking -> a small interactive example**. Preserve what changed in our understanding; let the next inquiry move follow from that.
