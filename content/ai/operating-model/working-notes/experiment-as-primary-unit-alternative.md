title: "Experiment As Primary Unit Alternative"
linkTitle: "Experiment As Primary Unit Alternative"
---
Teams should receive a problem and desired outcome, then discover a solution that is valuable, usable, feasible, and viable. Discovery explicitly involves rapid experimentation.

During uncertain AI discovery, the primary unit of work is the **experiment**. The capability funnel organizes evidence from those experiments. The feature becomes the primary unit only after the team has enough evidence to productize the approach.
