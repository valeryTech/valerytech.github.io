## 4. "MVP" becomes ambiguous


Avoid `MVP` in this framework unless it is explicitly qualified.

Marty has historically used MVP in the discovery sense as a test/prototype, while many engineering organizations use MVP to mean "first production version." Those are very different things.

Clearer terms are available:

```text
Discovery prototype
        ↓
productization commitment
        ↓
initial production slice
        ↓
subsequent production slices
```


For Wallet as an example:

```text
Discovery prototype:
Can one-shot natural language reduce capture effort?

Initial production slice:
Common, determinate expense capture.

Later slices:
Ambiguous expenses
Income
Transfers
Wider reference/language coverage
```


That vocabulary removes a lot of ambiguity.
