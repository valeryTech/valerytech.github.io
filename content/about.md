This is Valery Yuda's working technical notebook. It covers software engineering, system design, AI systems, cognition, and human-agent systems.

Across those topics, I am interested in task-solving systems: what they can do under specified conditions, how their parts interact, and how evidence and feedback help us evaluate and improve them. AI is one important part of that work, not the identity of the site.

My [Working Assumptions]({{< ref "ai-engineering/working-assumptions" >}}) page records the current view behind the AI notes, including what I mean by intelligence and why I treat LLMs as part of a larger search and selection process. It is timestamped and will change as the evidence changes.
